How to Capture an Accurate Leaf Photo

☀️

Natural Daylight

Photograph the leaf under clear daylight. Avoid harsh flash, shadows, or night captures.

🎯

Sharp Focus

Hold camera steady 15-25 cm from the leaf until spots, lesions, or veins are crisp.

🍃

Single Leaf

Place a single diseased leaf against a neutral surface or your palm for best AI recognition.

🔍

Check Underside

Check upper and underside of the leaf; powdery mildew and mites often reside underneath.
